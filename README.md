# Tatcha Digital Marketing Analysis 
This academic project analyzes customer reviews for the Tatcha skincare brand to identify consumer sentiment and evaluate the impact of pricing. 

## Objectives 
- Asses the impact of product price on ratings and recommendations
- Identify consumer trends and preferences
- Analyze consumer sentiment from product reviews
- Develop visualizations to communicate customer insights

## Tools 
- Python
- R
- Excel
- Tableau
- Canva

## Methods 
- A/B testing
- Sentiment analysis
- Data analysis
- Data visualization

## Key findings 
A/B Testing: 
Price A/B test: Higher-priced items slightly outperform lower-priced items in
average rating (4.26 vs 4.23)

Sentiment: 
Overall sentiment: 89.3% positive across all reviews
Highest-rated products:
Dewy Serum Resurfacing & Plumping Treatment (88.8%)
Mini Liquid Silk Canvas Primer (86.1%)
Mini Rice Wash Skin-Softening Cleanser (85.2%)
Rice Wash Skin-Softening Cleanser (85.2%)
Serum Stick: Treatment & Touch-Up Balm (84.4%)
